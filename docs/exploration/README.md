# Film simulation exploration

Reports of the steps from PoC to MVP. The steps and the evidence rules are in
[README section 8](../../README.md#8-route-from-poc-to-mvp). Source downloads, renders of the
source charts, and local plots stay under `build/tmp/` and `results/*/plots/` and are not
committed; each report states the commands that make them again.

| Step | Report | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | [Data and host spectral reference: Portra 400 and Endura Premier](p0_data_and_reference.md) | Measured 2026-10-29: the six Kodak charts are PDF vector paths and are digitized without manual tracing (tick residual at most 0.0007 density and 0.09 nm); the float64 chain from camera exposure to viewed print runs with finite output; the sources do not specify the pair completely (no isolated negative dyes, no negative density above 700 nm where 26 % of the paper red-layer printing weight is, no paper base, no paper exposure light); E-4050 is not self-consistent within the predeclared tolerances (speed point up to 0.12 log H, D-min up to 0.068 Status M); the uncalibrated neutral ramp stays within C*ab 3.6 (paper alone 3.4), but a 0.1 log H anchor error or another inferred cyan dye gives 12 to 13; the 0.1 nm reference is converged (0.0009 Delta E*ab against 0.05 nm); an independent hand digitization (spectral_film_lut) agrees to a median of 0.001-0.014; Kodak PDFs not distributed (U10); user review open |
| P1 | [Spectral input: AP1 to Portra 400 layer exposure](p1_spectral_input.md) (RGC out of scope, README U13) | Measured 2026-10-30: five homogeneous reconstructions (Mallett-Yuksel, Jakob-Hanika sigmoid, measured prior linear, non-negative QP, chromaticity-local) reproduce XYZ (Delta E*ab < 5e-6) and scale exactly (7e-14); none meets the layer tolerance (Status M p95 0.01) on any held-out dataset, and neither does the in-sample linear floor: the Portra red layer (651-680 nm) and blue layer (380-430 nm) are weakly constrained by XYZ (one grey XYZ allows red density 0.39-1.21; measured metamers differ by up to 0.06); skin has a systematic 380-430 nm bias (+0.02 blue) that a chromaticity-local prior trained on other subjects reduces 3-4 times; the assumed daylight reference light causes most non-daylight error (known scene light: 5-100 times smaller); camera IDT error is as large as the reconstruction error; the uncalibrated print shows Delta E*ab p50 4-7 on skin; non-negative QP covers narrowband light; revised criteria (U15) with no camera identity (U16): the camera population prior passes on skin and USGS for held-out makers (skin blue bias +0.039 to +0.005), natural objects and saturated patches fail p95; the 5-100 times gain needs the full light spectrum, a white point fixes only daylight and tungsten; selected route after U17 (final appearance is the priority): non-negative local prior with the near-black rule, reference light from the scene white (RAW) or ACES white (non-RAW); user review of appearance open |
| P2 | [Negative and print: appearance baseline](p2_negative_and_print.md) | In progress 2026-10-30: with a center-weighted exposure rule, a reflector display white, and an interlayer gain, the chain is near spektrafilm on 14 portraits (Delta E*ab p50 4.0, p95 16.5); saturated colours still clip on the display; most required P2 evidence is not measured |
| P3 | Scene presets and scanned-negative output | Not started |
| P4 | Spectral acceleration | Not started |
| P5 | Three GPU backends | Not started |
| MVP | Standalone workflow | Not started |
