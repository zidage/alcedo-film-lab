# Credits of the figures

The GPL-3.0 license of this repository does not apply to the photos in these figures. This repository holds
processed versions only. The RAW files are not redistributed.

Two photos are by Yurun Zi, the owner of this project (ROADMAP U66). They are street scenes in Hong Kong, and
they are here to show this project only. The other photos come from the free RAW collection of
[Signature Edits](https://www.signatureedits.com/free-raw-photos/), under its
[license and terms](https://www.signatureedits.com/free-raw-license-terms/). They are the work of their
photographers.

Selection rule for the Signature Edits files (ROADMAP U65): the terms do not grant the image of a recognizable
person, a trademark or logo, or a work of art in a photo. So these figures use landscapes and still lifes. Portraits were a main test
set of this project, and they stay local. If you own a right in one of these photos and want a change, open an issue.

Panels labelled spektrafilm are outputs of [spektrafilm](https://github.com/andreavolpato/spektrafilm) at
`3bb2c2d2` by Andrea Volpato (code GPL-3.0; profiles CC BY-SA 4.0), run as an external comparison.
